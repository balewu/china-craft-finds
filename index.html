BEFORE YOU BUY

A little note before you shop.

Hello~ this is how an order travels, who I am, and a few things I hope you will know first. Take your time (´▽`ʃ♡ƪ)

How an order moves

  1. 01

    You send a request here

    Nothing is charged yet, I promise. Your bag is only a little list of what you would like me to look for. ♡

  2. 02

    I check and quote the goods

    I look at each piece and see whether it can still be bought, then add the service fee and write to you with the amount for the goods.

  3. 03

    You pay for the goods

    Payment happens off this website, after you have seen the quote. I never ask for card details here.

  4. 04

    I buy them

    I order from the original shops. Little tools often come from several different sellers — that is just how this kind of hunt goes (•‿•)

  5. 05

    The parcels arrive with me

    I film the unboxing and check for anything missing or sent wrong. If a shop made a mistake, I talk to them myself, so you are not left waiting on a parcel that crossed the ocean and still came up short.

  6. 06

    I pack and measure postage

    Only when everything is here can I weigh and measure one parcel. Weight, size and your country all change the price, so I truly cannot name a shipping figure in advance. I pack as soon as the full order is in my hands.

  7. 07

    You pay the postage

    You will see the amount on the order once I have entered it. Until then it gently says “pending calculation”.

  8. 08

    I post it

    Once the postage is paid, I will send the parcel within five days. After it leaves my hands, customs and the carrier’s timetable are outside what I can promise.

Who I am

I’m a girl from China, and I have been making things with my hands for thirteen years. Perler beads, clay, resin, beading, weaving, wire wrapping — I have played with all of those, and plenty of other little crafts besides. I wanted to share the creative tools I genuinely find useful with makers all over the world, so their hands can work a little more easily and a little more happily. That is why I opened this small buying-for-you service. ♡

I am one person, buying things on your behalf. I am not a professional buying company, I do not make these products, and I am not the original shop. Please think of me as a craft friend who goes and fetches things.

If you are not sure how to use something, I am happy to explain it simply. What I cannot do — even though I wish I could — is undo what happens afterwards. If a piece is fragile, if a tool is damaged because it was used the wrong way, or if the result is not what you hoped for, that sits outside what I can put right. I am sorry about that in advance.

A lot of craft tools are consumable. A silicone mold lasts longer if you brush on a little mold release and unmold with a gentle hand, but it will still wear out one day. That is the nature of the tool, not a fault in your order.

What the service fee is for

The fee is not a surprise extra. It covers a few real costs of doing this by hand:

  • The fee PayPal charges on the payment.
  • Changing currency, and the cost of withdrawing it.
  • Postage from each original shop to me. The parts are often tiny and come from more than one seller. Opening them takes time: I record the unboxing, check that nothing was missed or sent wrong, and sort it out with the shop myself, so you do not spend weeks waiting on an overseas parcel that is missing something.

The prices you see on a product are item estimates only. The quote for the goods includes the service fee. International postage is a separate amount, later.

If you saw something else

If there is a craft tool, a piece of jewellery, or any small object you would like — something you saw on TikTok, or anywhere else — please tell me. I will go and look. If I can find a listing I trust, I can buy that for you too. ♡

Please also know

  • Colours on a screen, and the colours of handmade things, can differ a little from the photograph. I hope you will be kind about that small gap.
  • Once I have purchased the goods, the request generally cannot be cancelled. Please be sure before you pay — I will wait.
  • While a request is still “pending review”, you can cancel it yourself from your account. After I have started reviewing it, that button closes, because the work has already begun.
  • Fragile things can still crack on a long trip, even when I pack with care. I will do my best. I cannot promise every piece arrives unmarked.
  • When the goods have arrived with me, I will ask you to confirm the address. If it stays unconfirmed, I will not pack or post — not to be strict, but so nothing goes to the wrong door. Please glance at the city, street and postal code.
  • I keep your delivery details only so I can fulfil the order.
  • I cannot guarantee customs clearance, duties, or how long the carrier takes. If your country charges duties, those are yours to pay. I am sorry I cannot take that part on for you.

If this is not for you

If any of the above does not feel right, please choose another way to shop. I mean that warmly. People like different kinds of buying, and I respect that choice completely.

Thank you for reading this before you ask me to buy something. It means a lot. ♡
